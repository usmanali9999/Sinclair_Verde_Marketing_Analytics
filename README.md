## Executive Summary

Sinclair Verde Lamp Company is a Phoenix-based sustainable retailer that transforms discarded materials into decorative lamps.

The company needed to increase brand awareness, drive visits to its physical store, and strengthen online sales.

Working within a simulated agency scenario, I completed three connected business activities:

1. Developed an audience-driven internal marketing proposal.
2. Optimized keyword selection for an underperforming Google Ads Search campaign.
3. Analyzed annual and holiday marketing performance and presented business recommendations.

The project demonstrates how customer research, campaign forecasts, and performance data can support business decisions.

## 1. Business Problem

### Business objectives

- Increase brand awareness.
- Attract customers to the physical retail store.
- Improve online sales through digital marketing and Google Shopping optimization.
- Support a target of 3% growth in holiday online sales.

### Business context

- **Location:** Phoenix, Arizona
- **Products:** Upcycled lamps and sustainable home décor
- **Target market:** Adults aged 30–55 in the United States and Canada
- **Marketing budget:** $1,000 per month
- **Primary KPIs:** Lead conversion percentage and new customer acquisition

The company also wanted to improve its first-time online buyer percentage relative to first-time in-store buyers.

---

## 2. Customer-Persona-Driven Marketing Proposal

### Challenge

Determine which marketing strategies would most effectively communicate Sinclair Verde's environmental mission and reach potential customers.

### Analysis and decisions

The internal marketing proposal provided audience research indicating:

- **95%** of the proposal's 42–57 target demographic used at least one social media platform.
- **More than 50%** engaged with brand videos on social media.

Based on this research, I selected:

**Social Media Marketing**

Use content highlighting Sinclair Verde's sustainable products and environmental mission to improve awareness.

**Video Marketing**

Create short educational videos showing discarded objects being transformed into decorative lamps.

### Proposed content strategy

- Focus on educational content about upcycling.
- Distribute videos through YouTube and social media.
- Explain the environmental benefits of product reuse.
- Encourage viewers to explore Sinclair Verde's products.

### Business value

The proposal connected audience characteristics with channel selection and content strategy, providing a research-supported approach to brand awareness.

**Deliverable:** `01_Marketing_Proposal.docx`

## 3. Google Ads Keyword Optimization

### Campaign problem

The existing Google Ads Search campaign had been running for **30 days** with limited success.

**More than 90% of ad viewers were not clicking.**

The objective was to revise keyword selection to maximize projected website traffic without exceeding a **$1,000 monthly budget**.

### Approach

I evaluated the provided keyword options using:

- Predicted impressions
- Predicted click-through rate (CTR)
- Predicted clicks
- Predicted cost per click (CPC)
- Predicted total cost

### Selected keywords

| Keyword | Predicted clicks | Predicted cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crafts from recycling | 500 | $425 |
| Waste crafts | 225 | $90 |
| Crafts from waste | 500 | $425 |
| Environment crafts | 40 | $30 |
| **Total** | **1,265** | **$970** |

### Campaign forecast

**Predicted impressions:** 120,000  
**Predicted clicks:** 1,265  
**Predicted spend:** $970  
**Remaining budget:** $30  
**Blended projected CPC:** Approximately $0.77  
**Blended projected CTR:** Approximately 1.05%

### Business value

The revised keyword selection provided a quantified, budget-compliant traffic acquisition plan for a previously underperforming campaign.

The assignment suggested that keyword improvements could potentially increase clicks by up to 20%, but no actual post-optimization campaign results were provided.

**Deliverable:** `02_Google_Ads_Keyword_Optimization.xlsx`

## 4. Marketing Performance Analysis

### Objective

Evaluate the company's annual and holiday marketing performance, identify growth trends, and translate findings into recommendations.

### Organic search

Organic website traffic increased from **120,000 visits in January 2021** to **170,000 visits in December 2021**.

**Growth: 41.67%**

### Social media performance

The supplied case data showed:

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Social media mentions | 30% growth |
| Instagram followers | 64.29% growth, an increase of 9,000 |
| Twitter engagement | 90% growth |
| December Instagram followers | 23,000 |
| December Twitter engagement | 950 |

### Holiday season analysis

| Period | November organic traffic | December organic traffic | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2020 | 145,000 | 145,000 | 0% |
| 2021 | 165,000 | 170,000 | 3.03% |

The 2021 holiday period demonstrated positive organic traffic growth relative to the flat November–December performance in 2020.

**Note:** The 3.03% figure measures organic traffic growth, not online sales growth. The case data does not establish whether the 3% holiday sales target was achieved.

### Analytical contribution

Using Google's supplied reporting template and charts, I:

- Calculated and documented percentage changes.
- Interpreted website and social media trends.
- Compared holiday performance across two years.
- Identified business opportunities.
- Completed an 18-slide marketing performance presentation.

**Deliverables:**

- `03_Sinclair_Verde_Lamp_Company_Analysis_Report.xlsx`

## 5. Business Recommendations

Based on the case analysis:

**1. Strengthen organic search visibility**

Continue improving relevant content and search visibility to support sustainable website traffic growth.

**2. Prioritize audience-relevant content**

Use educational social media posts and videos to communicate the company's upcycling mission.

**3. Monitor paid search efficiency**

Evaluate keyword-level CTR, CPC, and clicks before allocating additional advertising spend.

**4. Improve conversion measurement**

Track the journey from website visits to product views, purchases, and new customer acquisition.

**5. Evaluate seasonal performance**

Use year-over-year and month-over-month comparisons to guide future holiday campaigns.
